Follow a tutorial to learn Facet by doing. Each one walks you through a job
in order, ends with a working result, and shows you the output to expect at
every step. Start at the top if Facet is new to you.

## In this section

- [Run Facet in 15 minutes](/tutorials/t1-run-facet/) — containers up,
  first admin seeded, signed in, welcome tour done.
- [Build your first domain](/tutorials/t2-first-domain/) — an Inventory
  domain with doctypes, fields, and a kanban board, built in the Builder.
- [Give your team access](/tutorials/t4-team-access/) — a colleague
  invited, a Sales role scoped to what they may see.
