<SectionHead eyebrow="What Facet holds" title="The records, the rules, and the memory">

The records are your documents in **domains** — clients, equipment, rentals.
The rules are **roles** that decide who reads and writes what, down to single
fields and rows. The memory is a full record of every change, with undo.

</SectionHead>

<LandingGrid>
	<Pillar kind="data" title="Data">
		Model the business in domains — clients, equipment, rentals — one slice at
		a time. Documents and files live in Facet, not in each app.
	</Pillar>
	<Pillar kind="permissions" title="Permissions">
		Roles decide who reads and writes, down to single fields and rows. The
		data layer enforces it, so no app can skip the rules.
	</Pillar>
	<Pillar kind="history" title="History">
		Every change lands in an append-only log: who changed what, when, and
		why. Roll any document back to a prior state.
	</Pillar>
</LandingGrid>

<SectionHead eyebrow="How it fits" title="Every app goes through the same gate">

An app never touches the database. It signs in over OAuth and calls the API
under the rights you gave it. Delete the app, and the data, the rules, and the
history remain.

</SectionHead>
