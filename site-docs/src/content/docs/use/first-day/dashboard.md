The dashboard is the page Facet opens after sign-in. It greets you, shows
your active role, and gives you cards to reach every domain you may read.
Each domain has its own dashboard, with your pinned views and your recently
opened records. Start your daily work here.

## Before you start

- **An account.** You need a Facet account. See
  [Sign in and secure your account](/use/first-day/sign-in/).
- **The right cards.** Your role decides which cards you see. A domain
  outside your rights does not show. See
  [Roles: who sees and does what](/discover/how-it-works/roles/).

## The home page

Open your instance address and sign in. The home page shows, from top to
bottom:

![Nadia's home page with the greeting, the key surfaces, and the domain cards.](/screens/use/first-day-tour-1.png)

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Greeting | Your first name, and the role you work under, for example *Manager*. |
| Key surfaces | Links to the areas your role allows, such as **Builder**. A link your role does not allow stays hidden. |
| Applications | One card per application your organization runs; the section hides when there are none. A card your role cannot open shows a lock notice. |
| Drafts in progress | The domain drafts you or your team work on, with a check mark when they are ready to publish. |
| Domains | One card per domain you may read, for example *Equipment* or *Rentals*. |

Select a domain card to open its dashboard.

:::note
When your role may create record types, the Domains section shows a small
plus button on each domain card. It opens the new-record-type dialog. Draft
and domain setup live in Builder; see [Build domains](/build/).
:::

When your account can read no domain, the Domains section shows a first-run
card instead: *"No domains available"*, with a note to contact your
administrator.

## The domain dashboard

The domain dashboard shows one domain: its record types, the views you
pinned, and the records you opened last.

![Nadia's Rentals domain dashboard: the header with counts, the pinned views, the recently viewed chips, and the record-type list.](/screens/use/first-day-dashboard-1.png)

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Header | The domain name, and counts of its record types and records. |
| Pinned views | One card per view you pinned, with the view type (table, board, or calendar) and the record count. Select a card to open the list. |
| Recently viewed | Chips for the records you opened last in this domain, newest first. |
| Record types | Every type with its record count, and links to its saved views. |

:::tip
Pin the views you use every day. Open a view on a list page and select the
pin control beside the tabs. The view then appears on this dashboard. See
[Lists, boards and calendars](/use/finding/lists-and-views/).
:::

## Steps

### 1. Open your daily list

Sign in, select a domain card, then select a pinned view. The list opens
with that view active. This is the shortest path to your daily work.

### 2. Return to a record you worked on

Open the domain dashboard and select a chip under **Recently viewed**. The
record opens. Each chip shows the record name and how long ago you opened
it, for example `10m` or `yest`.

### 3. Find a record type

Open the domain dashboard and read the record-type list. It shows every type
with its record count. A type with more than 1000 records shows `1000+`.

1. Select the type name to open its list.
2. Or select a saved view below it.

With more than six record types, the list shows the first six and a
**Show N more** button. Select it to see the rest.

## Example

A daily path through the Brightwater rentals domain:

```
/                      select the Rentals card
/rentals               select the Booking type, or its Calendar view
/rentals/Booking       the booking list opens — today's work starts here
```

## Troubleshooting

### A domain card is missing

Your role does not read that domain. Ask your administrator. A draft that a
colleague still works on stays out too; a published domain always shows.

### The Pinned views section is empty

You have not pinned a view yet. The section says: *"Pin a view and it will
show up here."*

1. Open a list.
2. Select a view.
3. Select the pin control.

### The Recently viewed section is empty

You have not opened records in this domain yet, or your browser cleared its
storage. The section says: *"Documents you open will appear here."*

### A count shows a dash instead of a number

The count has not loaded, or your role cannot count that record type. The
count fills in when the data arrives.
