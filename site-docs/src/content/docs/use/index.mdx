This tab is for everyone who works in the Facet workspace every day. It
covers the whole daily loop: sign in, find your records, edit them, see who
changed what, and ask the assistant to do it for you. Every page stands
alone, so start with the job you have now.

New to Facet? [The big picture](/discover/how-it-works/) explains the five
ideas behind the workspace in ten minutes.

## All pages in this section

**Your first day** — get into the workspace and know your way around:

- [A tour of the workspace](/use/first-day/) — where everything is.
- [Sign in and secure your account](/use/first-day/sign-in/) — sign-in, MFA, and password recovery.
- [The dashboard](/use/first-day/dashboard/) — the home screens and what each card shows.

**Work with documents** — the record loop:

- [Work with documents](/use/documents/) — the group at a glance.
- [Open and edit a document](/use/documents/view-and-edit/) — open a record, edit its fields, save, delete.
- [Comments and attachments](/use/documents/comments-attachments/) — discuss a record and attach files.
- [History and undo](/use/documents/history-undo/) — see who changed what, and restore an earlier state.
- [Import and export data](/use/documents/import-export/) — load records from CSV or JSON, and export what you see.

**Find things** — reach the record you need:

- [Lists, boards and calendars](/use/finding/lists-and-views/) — work with records in a table, kanban, calendar or grid, and save the view.
- [Search and the command palette](/use/finding/search/) — find records fast and run commands from the keyboard.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](/use/finding/keyboard-shortcuts/) — every shortcut, grouped by context.

**On your own**:

- [Your settings and API keys](/use/account/) — language, timezone, API keys, and signed-in devices.
