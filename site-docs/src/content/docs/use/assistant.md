You know what you need — "which tents are free in June" — and you would
rather ask than click through lists. The **assistant** is a chat window
that works with your records: it finds them, changes them, and takes you
to the right page. It runs inside your role, so it never sees or does more
than you could do yourself.

## Before you start

- [Sign in and secure your account](/use/first-day/sign-in/) — a signed-in session opens the assistant with your rights.

![The Assistant button in the top bar — selecting it opens the chat window.](/assets/screens/use/assistant-1.png)

## Open the assistant

Use any of these:

- Select the **Assistant** button in the top bar.
- Press `Ctrl+I` (`Cmd+I` on a Mac).
- Open the command palette (`Ctrl+K`) and select **Toggle the assistant**.

The chat opens as a floating window. Move it, resize it, collapse it, or
maximize it. It stays with you as you move through the workspace, so you
can keep it beside your work and ask as you go. Your organisation may
show another name than "Assistant"; it works the same way.

If there is no Assistant button, your role does not include assistant
access. Ask your administrator to enable it.

## What to ask

Type into the message box — it starts with "Ask about your documents…" —
and press `Enter` to send. Hold `Shift` and press `Enter` for a new line.

Questions that work well:

- "Find the companies with status lead."
- "Which tents are free on 14 June?"
- "Sum the annual revenue of these companies."
- "Change the status of Maison Lumière to active."
- "Open the booking BK-2026-0001."

The assistant replies in the language your workspace uses; you can ask it
to answer in another language.

## Follow the answer

The reply arrives while the assistant works. A **Thought** block shows what
it is checking, and folds away once the answer starts. Results appear as
record cards, compact rows, or number tiles. When the assistant opens a
page for you, the workspace goes there at once.

While the answer is running, the send button becomes **Stop**. Select it
to cancel — the text written so far stays, marked *Cancelled*. If you close
the window while it works, the answer finishes anyway; reopen the chat and
it catches up.

## Keep your conversations

The rail on the left lists your conversations. Select one to continue it,
select **+ New** to start another, and use the toggle in the rail header to
see archived ones. A finished answer carries a copy button.

## What it can do — under your role

The assistant can:

- find and read records, in one domain or across all of them
- show records as cards in the chat
- create, change, and delete records — a delete sends the record to the
  trash
- run the named actions your domain author defined
- count, sum, and average — and give the smallest and largest value
- open pages in the workspace
- read a record's history and tell you what changed

Every one of these runs as **you, through the role you are signed in as**:

- The assistant sees only what your role may see. A field your role cannot
  read stays invisible to it; a record outside your reach stays out.
- Switch roles, and the next message runs under the new role.
- Every change it makes is recorded in the record's history under your
  name — the same history as your hand edits, marked as done through the
  assistant.
- A refusal is final. When the assistant will not do something, it is
  because your role does not allow it; asking again does not change the
  answer. Ask your administrator, or do it yourself if you can.

This is the same rule on every way into Facet — see
[The assistant: AI with your rights](/discover/how-it-works/assistant/)
for the plain-words version.

## Where the usage limit shows

When your account has a usage budget, a bar above the message box shows
how much of it you have used: "62% of the 30-day budget used". No budget,
no bar. The window rolls: usage older than 30 days stops counting.

When the budget is spent, the assistant stops taking new messages:

> You have used your full assistant token budget for this 30-day window.
> Ask an administrator to raise the budget, or wait for older usage to
> leave the window.

## Troubleshooting

### The Assistant button is missing

Your role does not include assistant access. Ask your administrator to
enable it. There is no chat on the Configure screens either — that is
normal.

### The assistant refuses an action

The refusal is final: your role does not allow it. Ask your administrator,
or do the action yourself if you can.

### The message says you used your full budget

Wait for old usage to leave the 30-day window, or ask your administrator to
raise the budget.

### The answer stopped halfway

The connection may have dropped, but the work continues. Reopen the chat —
it catches up and shows the finished answer. You cannot send the next
message until the current answer finishes.

### An error line appears

For example: "The turn failed." Read the error, send the request again. If
it fails every time, ask your administrator.
