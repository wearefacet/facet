A **document** is one record in Facet: one booking, one item, one client.
Everything you do with records day to day happens in this group. The pages
use the Brightwater Rentals example — Tom's bookings, Lucía's maintenance
tickets.

## What do you want to do?

**Open and change a record.** Start from a list, the dashboard, or search,
read the record page, edit its fields, and delete it when needed →
[Open and edit a document](/use/documents/view-and-edit/).

**Discuss a record and keep its files.** Comments sit beside every document
that carries them, with mentions and file attachments →
[Comments and attachments](/use/documents/comments-attachments/).

**Fix a mistake.** Every change stays on record. Read a document's history,
put an earlier version back, or restore a deleted record →
[History and undo](/use/documents/history-undo/).

**Move records in and out.** Export a list to a spreadsheet, change it, and
bring it back → [Import and export data](/use/documents/import-export/).
