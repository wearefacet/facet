This section is your guide to daily work in Facet. It covers signing in,
finding and editing records, lists and views, comments, history, search,
notifications, import and export, the assistant, and your personal settings.

Read the pages in order for a full tour, or jump to the task you have now.

## In this section

- [Sign in, MFA, reset your password](/use/first-day/sign-in/) — get into
  the workspace, set up a second factor, recover access.
- [The dashboard](/use/first-day/dashboard/) — the home screens and what each
  card shows.
- [Lists, filters and saved views](/use/finding/lists-and-views/) — work
  with records in a table, kanban or calendar, and save the view.
- [Viewing and editing a document](/use/documents/view-and-edit/) —
  open a record, edit its fields, save, and delete.
- [Comments and attachments](/use/documents/comments-attachments/) — discuss a
  record in the side panel and attach files.
- [History and undo (rollback)](/use/documents/history-undo/) — see who changed
  what, and restore an earlier version.
- [Search and the command palette](/use/finding/search/) —
  find records fast and run commands from the keyboard.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](/use/finding/keyboard-shortcuts/) — every shortcut,
  grouped by context.
- [Notifications](/use/notifications/) — the bell, what sends
  notifications, and how to mark them read.
- [Import and export data](/use/documents/import-export/) — load records from CSV
  or JSON, and export what you see.
- [Using the assistant](/use/assistant/) — ask the chat assistant to
  read, change and navigate your data.
- [Your settings, sessions and API keys](/use/account/) —
  language, timezone, API keys, and signed-in devices.
