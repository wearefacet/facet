You run a company whose records live everywhere but together: a spreadsheet here, a CRM there, a booking calendar nobody trusts. Facet is one home for those records, with apps your team uses on top. This section explains Facet in plain words, says honestly where it fits, and shows you how to try it in about an hour.

Every example in these pages uses **Brightwater Rentals**, a fictional event-equipment company near Lyon. Its people — Nadia, Tom, Lucía, and Ravi — face the same problems you do.

## Understand Facet

Start with [Why Facet](/discover/why-facet/): the problems it solves, and the one idea behind it — apps are disposable, your data is not.

Then read [The big picture](/discover/how-it-works/). Five short pages, one idea each: domains, documents, roles, apps, and the assistant. Reading them in order takes about ten minutes.

## Is it for me?

[Is Facet right for you?](/discover/is-it-right-for-you/) lists the good fits and the poor ones, with no selling. [Security and governance](/discover/security/) answers the questions an auditor or an IT lead asks. [Licensing and services](/discover/licensing-and-services/) tells you what you may do with the software and where to get help.

## Try it in an hour

[Your first hour](/build/first-hour/) walks you from an empty server to a working domain with your team signed in. You build the Brightwater *Equipment* domain yourself, step by step, and see each result on screen.

Already convinced? Compare Facet with the tools you know in [Compare](/discover/compare/).
